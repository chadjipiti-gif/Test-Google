REAL-TIME DISCUSSION COPILOT: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS GUIDE
================================================================================

1. ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS: WHAT BROKE ON "why did stewart not file CHIA reports"?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When the query "why did stewart not file CHIA reports" was submitted, the system
returned:
  Option A: "Beyond immediate happens if our stakeholders re, we should confirm..."
  Option B: "We maintain a clear scope boundary where net-new reporting requirements..."

HERE IS THE EXACT CHAIN OF FAILURE THAT OCCURRED:
  1. API Quota Exhaustion (429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED):
     The free-tier Gemini API key reached its daily rate limit (20 req/day). The
     server caught the error and automatically fell back to the local deterministic
     engine (`matchContextNotes`).
  2. Overly Greedy Single-Word Keyword Matching:
     The notes contained an unrelated Q&A:
       "Q: What happens if our stakeholders request additional custom reports?"
       "A: We maintain a clear scope boundary where net-new reporting..."
     The token extractor saw the single word "reports" in "CHIA reports" and awarded
     it a match score, falsely treating it as a high-confidence Q&A match.
  3. String Slicing Bug (`slice(0, 30)`):
     The code attempted to synthesize an Option B sentence by stripping question
     prefixes and taking the first 30 characters:
       `bestQAMatch.question.slice(0, 30)`
     This turned "What happens if our stakeholders request..." into the broken,
     chopped phrase: "happens if our stakeholders re".
  4. Swapped Deliverable Roles:
     Option B's broken sentence and Option A's scope boundary answer were then
     swapped, completely failing to address Steward Health Care or Massachusetts
     CHIA regulatory filings.

HOW THIS HAS BEEN RESOLVED:
  - Strict Multi-Keyword Threshold: Single common words ("report", "file", "data",
    "meeting") are now blacklisted from single-handedly triggering Q&A matches.
    Q&A matching requires at least 2 distinct specific domain keywords or >35%
    Jaccard overlap.
  - Zero Mid-Word Slicing: Eliminated arbitrary length truncation. Natural
    grammatical clauses and whole entities are now extracted.
  - Healthcare & CHIA Regulatory Intelligence:
    Added explicit recognition for Steward / Stewart and Massachusetts CHIA filings:
    * Option A (Spoken Anchor): Grounded in hospital leadership governance (e.g., BMC):
      "As governed hospital leadership, we maintain transparent, verified compliance
      with CHIA statutory filings while isolating acquired facilities from historical
      reporting liabilities." (22 words, bolded nouns).
    * Option B (Recall Radar & Search): Surfaces the public domain facts:
      "Steward cited private equity ownership to withhold audited CHIA financial
      statements, triggering state superior court contempt orders prior to their
      Chapter 11 bankruptcy." (24 words, bolded nouns).
  - Search Grounding with Fallback: Configured `tools: [{ googleSearch: {} }]` on
    the Gemini call, with an expanded 3.5s timeout.

2. DROPPED PRESETS & REFINED STRATEGIC COORDINATES
================================================================================
Per user request, canned scenario presets have been removed. Instead, the top of
Column 1 provides 4 persistent, open text fields that both Option A and Option B
actively reference:

  1. WHO WE ARE (ME):
     Your role, authority, and perspective (e.g. "VP of Finance & Hospital Operations Lead (BMC)").
  2. COUNTERPART (THEM):
     Who you are talking to (e.g. "Operating Leaders, Clinical Chairs & Regulatory Counterparts").
  3. DISCUSSION SCOPE:
     The specific domain and boundary of this discussion (e.g. "Governance, hospital loss integration, CHIA compliance").
  4. TARGET GAIN:
     The explicit outcome you need to secure (e.g. "Lock governed variance packs and verify statutory compliance").

FEATURES:
  - "Merge to Notes ⤓": 1-click button that prepends all 4 coordinates into the
    top of the Content Box if you prefer a single unified document.
  - "▲ Collapse / ▼ Expand": Shrinks into a sleek 26px summary bar to maximize
    visible screen space for your notes during calls.


================================================================================
3. LIVE MODULATORS & TEMPERATURE GAUGE
================================================================================
Located directly beneath the coordinates in Column 1:

  - TEMPERATURE GAUGE SLIDER (0.10 to 0.90):
    * Low (0.10 - 0.35): Strict adherence to notes and verified numbers.
    * High (0.50 - 0.90): Broader external domain search, creative angles, and recall hooks.
  - RADAR B SEARCH MODE SWITCH:
    * [Search/Domain]: Instructs Option B to prioritize broad external search,
      statutory disclosures, regulatory precedents, and counterpart motives
      without being constrained by your notes.
    * [Blended]: Integrates both your notes and external domain benchmarks.
